# Pedro Alexandrino

**Founder · Developer · Finance Enthusiast**  
💡 *“Crafting logic and elegance in every line of code.”*

Hi, I'm Pedro Alexandrino — a Controlling and FP&A analyst with a curiosity that never quite settled down. Self-taught by origin, the kind of person who learns out of necessity, out of curiosity, and sometimes just because the subject exists.

I built my foundation in finance inside companies with a strong culture of technology and data — environments that taught me that well-structured financial processes and intelligent automation are not separate things. FP&A, management closing and controlling became my field; Python and data became my way of working within it.

Today I operate at the intersection of Controlling and technology — structuring analyses, automating routines and turning financial data into decisions. I think in systems before I think in tasks: what's causing the problem, how the parts connect, where the right lever is. I'm finishing my degree in Accounting with certifications in Valuation, Power BI and Pricing, deepening both the technical side and the business perspective.

What drives me at the core is being a polymath — not just learning different fields, but understanding how knowledge itself works, how things connect at the root. I build mentally before I execute, prefer depth over volume, and am more interested in understanding the structure of reality than in following the most obvious path. The destination is a business laboratory, a holding, projects in areas I don't even know will exist yet. Not every piece of knowledge has immediate utility — and that's fine. Sometimes you only find out something wasn't useful after you've learned it. That's how it works.
